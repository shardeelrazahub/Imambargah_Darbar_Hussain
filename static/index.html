About Our Imam Bargah

Markazi Imam Bargah Darbar Imam Hussain (A.S.)

Markazi Imam Bargah Darbar Imam Hussain (A.S.) is located in Memon Muhalla, Darbello New, Darbello, Pakistan. It serves as a central spiritual sanctuary for religious majalis, Azadari, Azadaran-e-Hussain (A.S.) gatherings, and community events throughout the year.

We welcome all believers and visitors to join in faith, remembrance, and community service.
